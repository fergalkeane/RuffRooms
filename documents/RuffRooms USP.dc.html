RuffRooms.ie Why we win

Ireland has thousands of places that accept dogs. Almost none of them tell you what that actually means.

A dog owner planning a week in Connemara doesn’t need another list of hotels with a paw icon beside the name. They need to know the fee is €15 and not €50, that a 32 kg Labrador won’t be turned away, that the garden gate actually latches, and whether the two of them can eat dinner together. Today that sits in a PDF, contradicted by a booking site, or is discovered on arrival.

RuffRooms.ie is the only platform in Ireland built around that gap. Every listing carries a structured, property-confirmed dog policy: charge, maximum number, size and breed limits, indoor access, facilities, re-confirmed every 90 days and scored as a single Ruff Score. Reviews come only from guests with a verified dog profile, so ratings reflect what happened when a real dog walked in. We take no commission and hold no payments.
